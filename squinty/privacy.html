Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Who we are

Squinty is an AI marketing and automation agency. This policy explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you book an audit we collect your name, your work email and, if you choose to give it, your website address. We use these only to arrange and carry out your audit and to follow up about it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we are in touch about your audit, and for up to 12 months afterwards unless you become a client. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We use a form provider to receive enquiries, which processes your details on our behalf.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Contact

To make a request, email us at the address on our website.
