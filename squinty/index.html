What actually happens in the free audit?

A 30 minute call where we go through your marketing, your website and how enquiries are handled. You leave with a short list of the biggest leaks and what fixing them would be worth, whether or not you work with us.

Is this just AI doing everything?

No. AI does the heavy lifting: testing, drafting, replying, reporting. Real people set the strategy, check everything before it goes live and are on the end of the phone. You get the speed of AI with the judgement of a team.

Do I need a big budget?

No. We work with small businesses, so we start with whatever will move the needle fastest at your size and scale from there. The audit will tell you what is realistic.

Will the AI agents sound like a robot to my customers?

They are trained on your tone of voice, your services and your prices, and they hand over to a human the moment a conversation needs one. Most customers simply notice they got a helpful reply fast.

Am I tied into a long contract?

No long tie ins. We work month to month after the initial build, because we would rather keep you with results than with paperwork.
