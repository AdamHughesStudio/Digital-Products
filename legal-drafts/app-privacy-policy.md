## The short version

- PlusFore helps golfers in the UK and Ireland find games, competitions and each other. To do that it
  keeps your profile, the games you post or join, your messages and the competitions you save.
- Other golfers see your profile (name, photo, handicap, home club if you choose to show it, the area
  you play in) and anything you post in a game or send them. Nobody sees your email address.
- If you let it, PlusFore uses your phone's location while you search, to show what's near you.
  That stays on your phone: we don't store it or show it to anyone.
- We never sell your details. We use analytics to improve the app and show adverts to keep it free,
  and we ask your permission before using anything that tracks you or personalises adverts.
- You can delete your account in the app at any time, which removes your profile, games, messages
  and everything else linked to it.
- Questions or requests: email [CONFIRM: support address, for example hello@getplusfore.app].

## What we collect

### When you create an account
- **Email address.** To sign you in with a one-time code and to contact you about your account. Never
  shown to other golfers.
- **Name and username.** Shown to other golfers.
- **Profile photo**, if you add one. Shown to other golfers.
- **Handicap**, and whether it has been verified. Shown to other golfers so games can be matched.
- **Home club**, if you add one. Shown to other golfers only if you choose to.
- **Short bio**, if you write one.
- **The area you play in and how far you'll travel.** You choose a town or area; we keep it and its
  map position to show games and competitions near you, and other golfers see the town.

### When you use PlusFore
- **Games** you post, request to join, are invited to or play in, with the tee time, course, price,
  spaces and any note you add.
- **Messages** you send in game chats and to friends, including GIFs, reactions and replies.
- **Friends**: requests you send and accept, and who your friends are.
- **Competitions you save.** Your friends can see that you're interested in a competition.
- **Reports and blocks.** If you report or block a golfer, we keep who, when and why so we can keep
  PlusFore safe.
- **Your current location, only if you allow it.** The first time you search, or tap the location
  button on the map, PlusFore asks whether it can use your location, and your phone asks you to
  confirm. If you agree, the app works out distances from where you are to games, opens and clubs.
  This happens on your phone: your location isn't sent to us, stored, or shown to other golfers,
  and it's forgotten when you close the app. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's
  settings, and you can always search from your chosen town instead.
- **Notification settings and a notification token** for each phone you sign in on, so we can send
  you the notifications you've turned on.

### Analytics
To understand how PlusFore is used and fix problems, we use [CONFIRM: analytics and crash reporting

which buttons are tapped, crashes, your device model and operating system, and an identifier for your
install. We use it to improve PlusFore, not to advertise to you. Where the law needs your consent for
this, we ask first, and you can change your mind in Profile, then Privacy.

### Advertising
PlusFore shows adverts, from [CONFIRM: ad network, for example Google AdMob], and clubs can pay to
promote their competitions (these are always labelled "Promoted").
- **Adverts that aren't personalised** use only basic information such as the app you're in and your
  rough area, worked out from your IP address.
- **Personalised adverts** use information about how you use apps and websites, and an advertising
  identifier, to choose adverts more likely to interest you. We only allow this if you agree. On
  iPhone, you're asked through Apple's "Allow tracking?" prompt; in the UK and Ireland you're also
  asked through our consent screen. You can change your choice at any time.
- We never give advertisers your name, email address, messages or the location from your phone.

### Information we don't collect
We don't collect your contacts, payment details or anything from other apps.

We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## Who can see what

- **Other golfers** see your public profile (name, username, photo, handicap, the area you play in,
  home club if shown, bio), the games you host and who's playing in them, and how many friends you have.
- **People in a game chat or a conversation** see the messages sent there.
- **Your friends** see which competitions you've saved.
- **Golfers you've blocked** can no longer send you friend requests, messages or game requests.

## How long we keep it

- **Your account and everything linked to it:** until you delete your account. Deleting it removes
  your profile, photo, games, requests, messages, reactions, friendships, saves, notification tokens
  and settings straight away.
- **Reports you made** stay, with your name removed, so we keep a record of how we handled them.
- **Reports about you** are kept for [CONFIRM: for example 2 years] after they're resolved.
- **Accounts banned for breaking our terms:** we keep enough to stop the same person signing up again,
  for [CONFIRM: for example 2 years].
- **Analytics:** for [CONFIRM: for example 14 months], then deleted or made anonymous.
- **Technical logs:** for the short period our providers keep them, usually no more than 30 days.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or
delete it, ask us to restrict or stop using it, and receive it in a portable format. You can delete
your account yourself in the app, under Profile, then Delete account.

For anything else, email [CONFIRM: support address]. We'll reply within one month, free of charge,
and may ask you to confirm who you are first.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also
complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

## Children

PlusFore is for people aged [CONFIRM: 16, or 13 with parental consent] and over. If you believe a
child younger than that has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

## Keeping your information safe

Everything is sent over encrypted connections, access to the database is controlled so golfers can
only see what this policy describes, and only the PlusFore team can see reports. No system is
completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share.

## Cookies and similar technology

The web app stores your settings and sign-in on your device. If we add analytics or adverts to the
web app, we'll ask before setting any cookie that isn't needed to make it work.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
