## 1. What PlusFore is

PlusFore helps golfers find people to play with, open competitions and championships to enter, and
information about golf clubs. We don't run the games or competitions themselves:

- **Games** are arranged between golfers. The host books the tee time with the club, and each golfer
  pays the club directly. PlusFore takes no payment and isn't part of that arrangement.
- **Competitions** are run by clubs and golf associations. Entries are made with them, on their own
  sites. We gather listings from clubs' and associations' published information and do our best to
  keep them accurate, but always check details with the organiser before you enter or travel.
- **Club information** such as photos and descriptions is for general guidance only.

## 2. Your account

- You must be 18 or over and live in the UK or Ireland to use PlusFore.
- Give accurate information, including your handicap, and keep it up to date.
- One account per person. Don't sign up as someone else.
- Keep your sign-in secure. You're responsible for what happens on your account.
- You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Profile, then Delete account.

## 3. How to behave

PlusFore only works if golfers can trust each other. You must not:

- harass, threaten, abuse, bully or discriminate against anyone;
- post or send anything hateful, sexual, violent, illegal or that you don't have the right to share;
- send spam, adverts or chain messages, or use PlusFore to sell things without our agreement;
- pretend to be someone else, or misrepresent your handicap, membership or ability;
- misuse members' guest arrangements against a club's own rules;
- try to access accounts or data that aren't yours, scrape the service, or interfere with how it works.

**We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users.** You can report any golfer or
anything they've sent from their profile or a chat, and block them so they can't contact you. We
review every report, aim to act within 24 hours, and may remove content and suspend or ban accounts
that break these terms, with or without warning.

## 4. Playing safely

Arranging to meet people you don't know carries some risk. Meet at the club, tell someone where you
are going, and use your own judgement. You're responsible for your own conduct on the course and for
following each club's rules, dress code and etiquette. We can't vet golfers and aren't responsible for
how anyone behaves when you meet.

You keep ownership of what you post (your profile, photos, game notes and messages). You give us a
non-exclusive, free licence to store, display and send it as needed to run PlusFore. You're
responsible for what you post and confirm you have the right to share it. We may remove anything
that breaks these terms.

## 6. Promoted listings

Some competitions are marked "Promoted", which means the club has paid for it to be highlighted.
Promoted listings are clearly labelled and are otherwise treated like any other listing.
