What happens when you join the waitlist

When you press Join the Waitlist, your browser sends the details you typed directly to Formspree, the service that collects our sign ups. This does not place a cookie on your device. Our privacy policy explains what happens to those details.

If this ever changes

If we add anything that uses cookies or similar technology and is not strictly necessary, such as analytics, we will ask for your consent first with a clear choice to accept or reject, and this page will become the place to change your mind at any time. We will also update the date at the top.
