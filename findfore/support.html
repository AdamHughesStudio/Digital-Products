Support

How can we help?

Answers to the questions golfers ask most, and how to reach us when you need a person.

Contact us

  • Email adam@adamhughes.studio with your question. We usually reply within two working days.
  • Tell us the email address you sign in with, the phone you use, and what happened. A screenshot helps.
  • To report a golfer or a message, use Report in the app so we see exactly what was sent. We aim to act on reports within 24 hours.

Signing in

I haven't received my sign-in code

Codes arrive by email within a minute. Check your junk or spam folder, and that the address is typed correctly. Codes expire after a short time, so if it's been a while, ask for a new one.

I've changed my email address

Email us from your old address if you can, with the new one, and we'll move your account across.

Games

How do games work?

A golfer posts a game with a course, tee time and the spaces left. Others ask to join and the host accepts. The host books the tee time with the club and each golfer pays the club directly. PlusFore takes no payment.

Someone hasn't turned up, or a game was cancelled

Games are arranged between golfers, so we can't refund green fees or book tee times. If someone behaved badly, report them from their profile.

Competitions

Where do the competitions come from?

Open competitions come from what clubs publish, and championships from the golf associations. Entry is always with the club or association, on their own site.

A date, fee or link looks wrong

Always check the club's own page before you enter or travel. If something in PlusFore doesn't match, email us the competition name and what's wrong and we'll correct it.

I run a club and want our opens listed, changed or promoted

Email us from the club's address with a link to your opens page. We can also add photos of your course that you're happy for us to use.

Safety

Reporting and blocking a golfer

Open the golfer's profile, or the chat, and choose Report or Block. Blocking stops them sending you friend requests, messages or game requests. You can unblock them from Profile, under Blocked golfers.

Meeting people you don't know

Meet at the club, tell someone where you're going and use your own judgement. You're never obliged to play with someone.

Your account and privacy

Changing what other golfers see

Go to Profile, then Privacy. You can choose what's shown on your profile, whether PlusFore uses your phone's location, and your analytics and advert choices.

Notifications

Go to Profile, then Notification settings, to choose what you're told about. If nothing arrives at all, check notifications are allowed for PlusFore in your phone's settings.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile, then Delete account. This removes your profile, photo, games, messages, friends and everything else linked to your account straight away, and can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address you sign in with and we'll delete it for you.

Your information

Our app privacy policy explains what we collect and why, and our terms of use set out how golfers treat each other on PlusFore.
