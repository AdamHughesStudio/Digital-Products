# PlusFore promo: design

Brand values from the app's `theme.ts` and brand guidelines.

## Colours

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Forest (canvas) | #0F1A14 |
| Forest raised (panels on the canvas) | #17271E |
| Sage | #6B8F7B |
| Sage deep (fills, buttons) | #577A67 |
| Light sage (accent, highlights) | #ADC8A2 |
| Mark sage (logo plus) | #8CA98B |
| Ivory (text on forest) | #E7E1DB |
| Cream (logo F) | #FBF7EE |
| Page (light panels) | #F2EEE8 |

## Type

Plus Jakarta Sans, 400 to 800, from `fonts/`. Headlines 800, labels 700 uppercase with wide tracking.

## Rules

- UK English. No em or en dashes anywhere in the copy.
- Real app screens only. No stock footage, no AI imagery.
- Call-outs: light sage rings on the screen, a drawn line, a raised forest label.
